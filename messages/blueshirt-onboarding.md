Welcome, new Blueshirt!

There are a couple rules you need to abide by and things you must do before we allow you into the server:

# Step 1
Please disable direct messages for the server, you can do this in the server settings, see screenshots below:

## If you're on Desktop:
**Part 1**
Right-click on the icon for this server and choose 'Privacy Settings'
**Part 2**
Disable both 'Direct Messages' and 'Activity Joining'
---
![Desktop Privacy Settings](images/onboarding/desktop-privacy-settings.png)
---
## If you're on Mobile:
Tap the server name in the header of the sidebar, then disable 'Allow Direct Messages'
---
![Mobile Privacy Settings](images/onboarding/mobile-privacy-settings.png)
---
# Step 2
Please change your nickname to your preferred personal name (not an online alias). This is under the 'Edit Server Profile' option in the same menu you accessed for Step 1.

# Step 3
It's important that you read [our Safeguarding policy](https://opsmanual.studentrobotics.org/about-the-charity/safeguarding) and abide by the following rules:

1. Do not ever private message or add a competitor to your Friends list, this is to ensure all communications are out in the open, and thus abide by the Child Protection laws. If competitors really need to speak privately, get their team supervisor (the adult who runs the team) to email [teams@studentrobotics.org](mailto:teams@studentrobotics.org)
2. If you join a private group chat or voice call with competitors, there must always be a team supervisor or supervisor-nominated adult present. Otherwise you should not be there!
3. You're representing Student Robotics, try not to give definite answers to questions which you don't 100% know the answer to. If you aren't sure, say "I'm not sure, but I think X might be true".


Please also read the rules in #welcome-and-rules.
