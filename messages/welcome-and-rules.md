## Rules for this Discord

__1. Do not Friend/Private Message a $blueshirt (volunteer)__ Instead, you can mention them in one of our text channels — or if it's a private matter, get your team supervisor to email us.
__2. No inappropriate content__ Keep everything to a 'Parental Guidance' :pg: rating. This includes profile pictures and usernames.
__3. Keep a respectful atmosphere.__ We're all here to have fun and learn. It's just a game, so please stay friendly and respectful.
__4. Public channels are English only.__ This is an international server, so we need to keep the conversation accessible to everyone. This does not apply to team channels.

Hopefully we won't need to, but we will raise violations of these rules to your team supervisors/institutions/schools.

## How to get access

You should have already been sent a welcome message by $bot, in your very own `#welcome-<yourname>` channel.

To get access, use the `/join` command with your **team password** (which was emailed to your team supervisor). Once you do this, you will automatically be given access.
